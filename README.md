# Snake Game - The Ball Can Run

## Link
https://thienquan-art.github.io/Snake-Game---The-Ball-Can-Run/

## Game Description

Snake, except the food runs away and really doesn’t want to be eaten.
Instead of eating food that sits still, you’re hunting a fast red ball that actively tries to escape. Every time you catch it, the arena shrinks, giving you less room to move and making each round more intense.

## Features

### Smart Prey
The red ball doesn’t just wait around to be caught. When you get close, it runs away. Keep chasing it and it may use a one-time burst of speed to escape toward a distant corner.

### Shrinking Arena
Every catch makes the playing area smaller. As the game goes on, you have less space to maneuver, making it harder to avoid crashing while staying on the prey’s tail.

### Sprint Boost 
Press Spacebar to give your snake a quick burst of speed. You can use it whenever you want, whether you’re closing in on the prey or trying to escape a dangerous situation.

## How to Play
### Objective
Control your snake, chase down the red ball, and catch it as many times as you can.

### Controls
Arrow Keys or W, A, S, D — Move up, down, left, or right
Spacebar — Activate a temporary speed boost
